About

House of Dermatology is founded upon a philosophy of integrity in our approach to aesthetic medicine. This manifests in evidence-based treatments, a timeless approach rather than following trends, and maintaining a long-lasting relationship with our patients throughout their aesthetic journey. We understand that the focus of your aesthetic treatments will change as you age, and we are here to support and guide you throughout these natural stages.

Your House of Dermatology Care Team

Dr Jean-Paul Chung

Medical Director

B.Med, M.Trauma, FACCSM, FCPCA.

A specialist in the art of refined restoration, Dr Jean-Paul blends a foundational background in trauma surgery with a master’s eye for facial architecture. As a dual Fellow of the Australasian College of Cosmetic Medicine and Surgery (ACCSM) and Cosmetic Physicians College of Australasia (CPCA), he is a leading voice in cosmetic innovation, frequently sharing his expertise on global stages and at national conferences to shape the future of aesthetic medicine.

Jean-Paul’s practice is defined by "The House of Dermatology Standard": an uncompromising commitment to clinical excellence. As a field expert for the Aesthetic Medical Emergency Team (AMET), he provides high-level guidance on complex cases, while his pioneering use of ultrasound technology ensures an unparalleled level of safety.

He is a clinical investigator and has served on the advisory boards of cosmetics and pharmacology companies. He is also a brand ambassador and key opinion leader for multiple leading laser, cosmetic and bio-rejuvenation companies.

Specializing in the sophisticated application of advanced laser systems and energy-based devices, he crafts bespoke, minimally invasive journeys that prioritize full-facial rejuvenation, enhancement and scar treatments.

Fluent in both English and French, Dr Jean-Paul delivers ethical, holistic results - achieving a timeless, natural elegance that is uniquely your own.
